WHAT IS AUTISM?

Understanding Autism

Autism is a neurodevelopmental condition that affects how a child communicates, interacts with others, experiences the world and learns. It does not look the same in every child and children can have a wide variety of developmental needs, including those associated with autism, ADHD, Down syndrome and other forms of neurodivergence. Understanding each child’s individual development is the first step towards providing the right support.
