Recognising Early Differences

Connecting with others feels different

Children may respond differently to their name, make less eye contact or find social interaction more challenging.

Sensitive to everyday sensations

Autistic children may be more or less sensitive to sounds, lights, textures, touch or other sensory experiences.

Comfort in routine

Many autistic children prefer familiar routines and may find unexpected changes or transitions difficult.

Speech develops differently

Speech may develop later than expected or a child may communicate using words, gestures, sounds or other forms of communication.
