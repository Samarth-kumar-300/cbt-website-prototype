THE EVIDENCE

Supporting inclusive education with evidence that matters

Clinically backed

Developed through autism intervention research and evaluated through real-world clinical implementation, with research demonstrating measurable improvements across developmental domains.

Government validated

A study by the National Institute for the Empowerment of Persons with Intellectual Disabilities (NIEPID) validates CognitiveBotics as “an effective adjunct to conventional therapy, with measurable improvements in developmental and social domains.”

Patent protected

Built on proprietary AI technology designed specifically for early intervention, personalised learning and developmental support (Indian Patent No. 586929).

Designed around measurable outcomes

A 12-month observational study demonstrated statistically significant improvements across multiple developmental domains when CognitiveBotics was used consistently alongside existing intervention programmes.
